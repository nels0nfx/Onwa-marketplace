# Legal and compliance notes (not legal advice)

Nothing here certifies compliance. Obtain Nigerian legal, tax and payment-provider advice before commercial launch.

- **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**: determine NDPC registration/filing duties, lawful basis, a privacy notice, consent records, data-subject access/correction/deletion processes, retention schedule, breach notification (72h) procedure. v0.1.0 stores name, email and phone only, and the sign-up form references the NDPA but no privacy notice text exists yet.
- **Consumer protection, seller verification, tax**: to be designed with the seller and checkout stages.
- **Payments**: an internal ledger or split does not make ONWA a licensed escrow provider or financial institution. Do not describe held funds as escrow without an approved legal and provider structure. Confirm Paystack account, settlement and split requirements before live money (Stage 5).
- **Restricted product categories**: define the prohibited/regulated list before catalog launch.
- **Incident response**: write a runbook before production.
